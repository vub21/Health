Thicker Hair Guide
Pattern hair loss, explained plainly

Thicker strands, more hairs.

Every food, medicine, device and habit is listed once, with what it does, how it is used and what to watch for. Tap a goal to see only what helps with it.

Where to start

  1. Get testedAsk for ferritin (iron stores) and vitamin D. Fix a real shortage before anything else.
  2. Pick a proven treatment with a doctorMinoxidil and finasteride have the strongest evidence. Add food and habits around them.
  3. Give it six monthsHair grows slowly. Guidelines check results at around 6 months.

What do you want to change?

Thicker strands means each hair gets wider. Regrowth means more hairs per square centimetre.

Read this first

This guide is general information about pattern hair loss, not medical advice. Sudden shedding, patchy loss or a sore scalp needs a doctor. Figures from the original notes that could not be confirmed, such as exact percentages, laser schedules and a vitamin D target, were left out. Evidence bars show how well each item is supported for hair, not how healthy it is in general.
