Gym Week Plan

Gym Week

3 strength days, 2 cardio days, 2 recovery days. Each session runs 60 minutes from the moment you walk in.

Rest between sets

Exercise typeRest
Leg press, squats, Romanian deadlifts90–150 s
Chest press, rows, lat pulldown90–120 s
Shoulder press90–120 s
Curls, triceps, lateral raises60–90 s
Planks and core45–60 s
  • These are rests between sets of the same exercise. Moving to a new machine takes about 1–2 minutes extra for setup.
  • Pick a weight that leaves 2–3 reps in reserve. Good form comes before heavier weight.
  • If a machine is busy or you need more rest, finish the strength work first and trim the cardio.
  • Stick with this for 6–8 weeks before changing anything big.
